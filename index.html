What counts as a pricing error?

A listing dramatically below comparable prices may be a clearance sale, typo, outdated price, scam or genuine mistake. The board labels these possible pricing errors until confirmed. Retailers may cancel orders or refuse to honor erroneous prices.

How updates work

Deals are loaded from deals.json. The daily ChatGPT roundup is scheduled separately. Automated collection and publishing to this board are not yet connected; only supported sources and verified offers should be added.
